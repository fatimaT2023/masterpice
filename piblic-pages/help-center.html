Account & registration

How do I create a parent account?

Open the registration page and choose Parent. Enter your name, email and phone number, choose a password that meets the requirements, confirm it and accept the terms. After registration, follow the steps to set up your child's profile.

What should I do if I can't complete registration?

Check the error message below each field. Make sure your email is correct, both passwords match, all required fields are complete and you have accepted the terms. If you still need help, contact support below.

I received an invitation from an admin. How do I start?

Open the registration link in your invitation and create a parent account. The invitation guides you to registration; you choose your own password. You do not need to share your password with the admin.

What if I forgot my password?

On the login page, choose Forgot password and follow the instructions. If you cannot continue, contact support. Never share your password or verification codes.

Child profiles

How do I add a child or switch between children?

After logging in, complete your child's profile setup. To add another child, open the child menu at the top of the page and choose “Add another child”. You can also use this menu to switch between your children's profiles.

How do I update my child's details?

Select your child's name at the top of a parent page to open their profile, then use the available editing options. Make sure you have selected the right child before making changes.

Bookings & specialists

How do I book an appointment with a specialist?

After logging in, open “Specialists” and choose “Find a specialist”. Review the specialist's profile, open the booking page, choose an available appointment and check the details before confirming.

Where can I find my appointments and specialist reports?

Open “My appointments” from the Specialists menu, or “Visits & reports” from Records. Select an appointment to review its details and report, when available.
