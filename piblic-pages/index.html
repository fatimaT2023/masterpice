    My son is 4 and keeps hitting other children. How should I respond?

    Hitting is common at this age, when big feelings arrive faster than the words for them. Stay calm, name the feeling, and show what to do instead: "Hands are for gentle touches."

    Suggested next steps

    • Step in early and move him away from the situation.
    • Note when it happens for a week to spot triggers.
    • Open the 4–6 guide on Aggression.

    General guidance. Not a diagnosis.

        Trust & AI safety

        Built with safety at the center.

        AI does not diagnose

        Rafiq explains and suggests. It never names a condition for your child.

        Screening results are not medical diagnoses

        They point to what may need a closer look, nothing more.

        Urgent red flags trigger emergency guidance

        Normal answers pause and you are directed to urgent care.

        Child information is treated as sensitive data

        Profiles and health notes are handled with extra care.

        AI Knowledge & Safety

        Rafiq is designed to use reviewed, trusted health and child-development information.

        • Reviewed content. Guidance comes from material that people on the team have reviewed, with admins managing what the AI can draw on.
        • Clear limits. Every answer is general and educational. When a question needs a professional, Rafiq says so.
        • Always a next step. Concerns lead to practical actions, a screening, or a specialist you can book.

        No AI system is perfect. Rafiq supports your judgment and does not replace a doctor.
