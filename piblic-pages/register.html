Privacy & Terms

Important information about using Rafiq.

Privacy

Privacy Policy

  • Rafiq may collect basic account information such as name, email and phone number.
  • Parents may provide child information such as age, development records, screening responses and health notes.
  • This information is used to personalize the Rafiq experience and provide relevant platform features.
  • Child-related information should be treated as sensitive.
  • Information related to a consultation may be shared with the selected specialist when necessary.
  • Rafiq does not sell users' personal information.
  • Users may request account and data deletion.
Child data is sensitive.

Only provide information needed for the Rafiq features you choose to use.

Terms

Terms of Use

  • Users should provide accurate information.
  • Users are responsible for protecting their account credentials.
  • Rafiq provides parenting and child-development support tools.
  • Specialists are responsible for the professional information and services they provide.
  • Misuse of the platform or attempting to access another user's information is prohibited.
  • Accounts that violate platform rules may be restricted.
Use Rafiq responsibly.

The platform is designed to support families and specialists, not to enable misuse or unauthorized access.

Safety

Medical Disclaimer

Rafiq is not a substitute for professional medical care.
  • Rafiq AI provides educational and supportive guidance only.
  • AI does not provide medical diagnosis, prescriptions or emergency medical services.
  • Screening results are not a diagnosis.
  • Parents should seek professional evaluation when they have concerns about their child.
  • In an emergency, users should seek urgent medical care or contact local emergency services.
  • Automated systems may not detect every emergency situation.
