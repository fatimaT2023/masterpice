Before you write.

Can I book or pay for a real session?

Not in this version. Booking and payment are demonstrations; no money is collected and no live appointment is created.

Which ages can use the current experience?

The current personalized coverage is birth to under 7. Older ages are part of our future plans.

What if I have trouble registering?

Check the field messages, email spelling and password confirmation. The Help Center has a step-by-step guide.
