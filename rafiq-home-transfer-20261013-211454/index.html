Questions before you start?

A few things parents and specialists usually want to know.

Which ages does RAFIQ support?+

RAFIQ follows your family from birth to age 18, with guidance shaped around each stage.

Can I explore my child's stage?+

Choose an age on the growth timeline, or explore the parent dashboard preview.

Does AI guidance replace a professional?+

No. AI guidance and screening support informed next steps. They do not provide a medical diagnosis or replace professional care.

How can specialists use RAFIQ?+

The specialist workspace brings profiles, availability, bookings, consultations, reports and follow-ups together.

How do I get started?+

Explore the previews or use the account-access buttons. Registration, payments and live specialist services are not connected in this homepage preview yet.
