Privacy

Review how this RAFIQ prototype handles stored information.

◇

Prototype Data Storage

This frontend prototype stores demo child profiles, bookings and tracking information locally in the browser. A production platform would use authenticated backend storage and appropriate security controls.

Privacy Policy+

This preview stores account preferences, child records and photos in this browser. Clearing browser data can remove them. Download copies of important records and photos before clearing site data.

Cloud storage is not connected. This prototype does not provide account-based privacy protection for data stored on this device.

Terms of Use+

This is a demonstration of Rafiq. Account, family and subscription features use local browser data. Payments and invitations are simulated; no real payment or invitation email is sent.

Use demo information when exploring this preview. Production terms are not available in this prototype.

Medical Disclaimer+

Rafiq provides educational content and a place to organize your observations. It does not provide a diagnosis or replace professional medical advice.

Saved observations and health records are information you enter. Discuss developmental concerns and vaccination plans with a qualified healthcare professional.
